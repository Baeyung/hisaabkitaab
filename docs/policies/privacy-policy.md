# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 27 July 2026
**Last Updated:** 27 July 2026

Hisaab Kitaab ("Hisaab Kitaab," "we," "us," or "our") provides a business management platform for shopkeepers, wholesalers, and traders — including cashbook, inventory, khata (party ledger), billing, and analytics tools ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Hisaab Kitaab, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Who We Are

Hisaab Kitaab is operated from Faisalabad, Pakistan. For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at **support@hisaabkitaab.shop**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** name, business name, phone number, email address, and password.
- **Business data you enter:** cashbook entries, inventory records, customer/supplier khata (ledger) entries, invoices, and any other business records you create within the Service.
- **Support communications:** messages you send us for help or feedback.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage data:** pages/features accessed, actions taken, timestamps, device type, and app version.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, and operating system, collected for security and diagnostics.

### 2.3 Payment Information
When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed through **PayFast**, a payment gateway licensed by the State Bank of Pakistan. You are redirected to PayFast's own secure checkout page to complete payment, and redirected back to Hisaab Kitaab once done.

- We **do not collect or store** your card number, mobile wallet PIN, or bank account credentials on our servers.
- We receive only confirmation of payment status, a transaction reference ID, and the amount paid.
- Your payment details are handled entirely under PayFast's own privacy and security practices. We encourage you to review PayFast's privacy policy as well.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:
- Create and maintain your account and business records.
- Provide core features: cashbook, inventory tracking, khata management, billing, and analytics/reports.
- Process subscription payments and send billing-related notices.
- Send service updates, security alerts, and (if you opt in) product updates.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues.
- Improve and maintain the Service.

We do **not** sell your business data or personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Your Business Ledger Data

The cashbook, inventory, khata, and billing data you enter belongs to **you**. We act only as a custodian of this data to provide the Service. We do not access, read, or analyze your business records except:
- When needed to provide technical support you've requested,
- To maintain the security and integrity of the platform, or
- Where required by law.

---

## 5. How We Share Information

We share information only in these limited circumstances:
- **Payment processing:** transaction details shared with PayFast to complete your payment.
- **Service providers:** hosting, infrastructure, and analytics providers who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal requirements:** if required by Pakistani law, court order, or government authority.
- **Business transfers:** in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

---

## 6. Data Storage & Security

- Your data is stored on secured servers with access restricted to authorized personnel only.
- We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls, regular backups) to protect your information.
- No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; while we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

---

## 8. Your Rights

You may:
- Access, correct, or export your business data at any time through the app.
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
- Opt out of non-essential communications (e.g., marketing) at any time.

---

## 9. Cookies & Similar Technologies

If Hisaab Kitaab is accessed via a web browser, we may use cookies or local storage to keep you logged in and to remember basic preferences. These do not track you across other websites.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

Hisaab Kitaab is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email, and update the "Last Updated" date above.
